\begin{highlights}
\item Record-level coding of \Ncore{} medical foundation-model interpretability studies
\item More records here measure what representations encode than what models use
\item Same-data comparisons report selected properties in both medical and general models
\item In this corpus no controlled evidence identifies an effect of medical pre-training
\item Connector and text encoder almost unmeasured; dictionary stability rarely tested
\end{highlights}
